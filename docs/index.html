What it is

XSpaceWar-AI is a modern, AAA-styled networked space-fighter with a strong AI focus — a 2026 reimagining of the classic networked Spacewar! / xspacewar. Bird's-eye view like the 1962 original, but with space-accurate Newtonian physics, 100% procedurally generated graphics and audio (every sound synthesized from math at load), and LAN + internet multiplayer for up to 16 ships. Built in Godot 4. Free, Apache-2.0.

Features

  • Top-down arena dogfights around a gravity well, up to 16 ships.
  • Real Newtonian flight — conserved momentum, inverse-square gravity on ships and torpedoes, gravity-assist slingshots, hyperspace.
  • Procedurally generated everything — stars, planets, nebulae, ships, VFX, and audio; every match a fresh seeded arena.
  • Modes: Free-for-all, Team battle, AI bots (Rookie → Insane), and an all-AI Movie Mode attract.
  • Multiplayer today: host-authoritative netcode with client-side prediction + reconciliation, automatic LAN discovery, direct IP, and internet play via room codes and a server browser — no port forwarding required.
  • Seven UI languages — English, Spanish, French, Simplified Chinese, Arabic (RTL), and Hindi.

Heritage

Stands on one of gaming's oldest lineages — Spacewar! (MIT, 1962, PDP-1), the 1974 PDP-11 networked port, and Ron Frederick's 1992 X11 xspacewar 1.2. An original clean-room implementation.
